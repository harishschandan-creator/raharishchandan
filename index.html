Code of Conduct & Securities Dealing Procedures

Trading Blackout Periods

As a SEBI Registered Research Analyst, S Harish Chandan strictly enforces a mandatory 30-day pre-recommendation trading blackout and a 5-day post-recommendation trading blackout in any security or derivative contract covered in research reports.

Prohibition of Front-Running

Zero tolerance for front-running or personal trading ahead of issuing research reports. All personal transactions are subject to internal audit and regulatory scrutiny.

Arm's Length Separation

Complete separation from execution services, proprietary trading desks, or investment banking divisions to ensure objective, conflict-free research recommendations.

Conflict Disclosure

Any financial interest, holding, or material conflict of interest regarding subject companies is explicitly disclosed within each published research report.

Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & PMLA 2002 Policy

In accordance with the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002 and SEBI guidelines, S Harish Chandan maintains strict Client Due Diligence (CDD) procedures for all subscribers.

Client Identification Mandatory Aadhaar/address proof and verified bank account mapping prior to activating research service subscriptions.
Record Retention (10 Years) All financial transaction records, KYC documents, and research correspondence are securely archived for a minimum period of 10 years.
Independent Annual Audit Internal systems and AML compliance protocols undergo independent annual audits as mandated by SEBI.
